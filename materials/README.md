# Materials to prepare before any session

## Lecture videos (`videos/` and `videos.json`)

Ten clips, `V01.mp4` ... `V10.mp4`, about 3 minutes each.

- **Topic outside the students' syllabus**, so the quiz measures what they learned in the session, not what they already knew.
- **Slides with clear changes.** Write each slide-change time (seconds from the start) in `events`; the coupling detector checks whether faces react to them.
- **Same clips for every student.** Synchrony is computed between students who saw the same clip.
- Five multiple-choice questions per clip in `quiz` (`answer` = index of the correct option, starting at 0). Pilot them: a student who watched should score 3 or more out of 5, a student who did not should score 2 or less.

`videos.json` holds placeholders until you fill it in; the app reads `file`, `duration`, `events` and `quiz`.

## Word lists

Generated by `dg/protocol.py` (`word_lists()`), 10 lists of 12 everyday English nouns, fixed by a seed so every student gets the same list for the same part. Replace `WORD_POOL` there if English nouns are a poor fit for your students.

## Information sheet and consent

`information_sheet.md` and `consent_form.md` are drafts for the ethics application. Translate them into Urdu (and Pashto if needed) and have the translation checked before use. Under-18s need a parent's signature as well as their own assent.

## Thresholds that decide whether a part counts

In `dg/data.py`: genuine attention and faked distraction count only if the quiz score is at least 3 of 5; genuine off-task and faked attention count only if at least 6 of 12 words are recalled. Pre-register these before Study 1.
